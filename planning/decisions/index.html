  • Dates

    Leaders, settled

    Sat Apr 29 - Sat May 20, 2028 (22 nights), farewell Sun May 21. Kentucky Derby is Sat May 6, day 8.

  • Route and nights

    Leaders, settled

    Bowling Green & Mammoth Cave 3 -> Louisville 2 -> Frankfort 3 -> Lexington 6 -> Berea & Renfro Valley 8. West to east, no backtracking.

  • Derby plan

    Colleen, settled

    No Derby at Churchill Downs (cost). Pre-Derby race day at Churchill Downs Wed May 3; watch the Derby at Keeneland's Derby Day, as the 2012 caravan did.

  • Official club caravan

    Leaders, settled

    An Airstream Club International national caravan.

  • Caravan name and number

    Steve, open

    'Springtime in Kentucky' is an existing ACI caravan (N-2023-Y to N-2025-Y, other leaders). Ask the Caravan Committee chair before using the name.

  • Group size and rig limits

    Leaders, open

    The 2023-25 runs capped at 26 rigs including leaders. Every campground pick is judged against that.

  • Campgrounds

    Leaders, open

    First calls: Bowling Green KOA, Louisville South KOA, Elkhorn Creek RV Park, Kentucky Horse Park, Renfro Valley KOA. See Campgrounds.

  • Farewell venue

    Leaders, open

    Renfro Valley KOA pavilion, Fort Boonesborough activities building (seats 150) or Boone Tavern.
