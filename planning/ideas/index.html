Bowling Green & Mammoth Cave Apr 29–May 2

  • shortlist Mammoth Cave tours
    Pick two: Historic Tour plus Domes and Dripstones, or the lantern-lit Violet City tour. Reserve on Recreation.gov.
    source
  • idea Hidden River Cave and American Cave Museum
    One ticket covers both, in Horse Cave. Check current hours and prices.
    source
  • idea 2012 did National Corvette Museum
    2012 did the museum and the assembly-plant tour.
  • idea 2012 did Lost River Cave boat tour

Louisville May 2–May 4

  • scheduled Pre-Derby race day at Churchill Downs
    Wed May 3, 2028. In 2026 the Wednesday of Derby week was 'Winsday'. 2012 did the same with a Derby Museum tour.
    source
  • shortlist Bernheim Forest
    Giant troll sculptures and a treetop canopy walk, about 15 minutes from the Shepherdsville campgrounds.
    source
  • idea Great Steamboat Race
    Ran the Wednesday before the Derby in 2026; that would be the same day as our race day. Date inferred.
    source
  • idea 2012 did Louisville Slugger Museum and lunch cruise

Frankfort May 4–May 7

  • scheduled Buffalo Trace Distillery, Trace Tour
    Free, 75 minutes, the only accessible tour. Email for ~50 people in four groups of 12-13. Trucks only; carpool. 2012 toured it too.
    source
  • scheduled Derby Day at Keeneland
    Sat May 6, 2028, about 30 minutes from Frankfort. Colleen's call for cost; 2012 did the same.
    source
  • idea Kentucky History Center and Old State Capitol
    One ticket covers both plus the Military History Museum. The new Capitol's interior is closed for renovation until about Jan 2029.
    source
  • idea Castle & Key Distillery
    Restored 'castle' distillery; book ahead.
    source
  • idea 2012 did Rebecca Ruth bourbon-ball candy tour

Lexington horse country May 7–May 13

  • shortlist Working horse-farm tours
    Booked through Horse Country, Inc. (e.g., Claiborne, Lane's End). Group tours ran about $20 in 2020.
    source
  • shortlist Kentucky Horse Park museum and Hall of Champions
    Right where we camp.
  • idea Camp Nelson National Monument
    Civil War camp where many Black Union soldiers enlisted. Check May hours (daily hours began Memorial Day in 2024).
    source
  • idea 2012 did Shaker Village of Pleasant Hill
  • idea 2012 did Old Friends retired thoroughbred farm

Berea & Renfro Valley May 13–May 21

  • shortlist 2012 did Kentucky Artisan Center and Berea galleries
  • shortlist 2012 did Renfro Valley shows
