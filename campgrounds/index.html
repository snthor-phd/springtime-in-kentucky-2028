Bowling Green & Mammoth Cave Apr 29–May 2, 3 nights

First callcandidate

Bowling Green KOA Holiday

(270) 843-1919 · Reservations (800) 562-2458

1960 Three Springs Rd, Bowling Green, KY

Sites
148 sites; full-hookup pull-throughs, 30/50 A; up to 100 ft
Groups
Conference center seats 75+ banquet-style; pavilion. ACI caravans started here 2023-25.
Drive
About 40 min to the Mammoth Cave visitor center (I)
Reviews
RV LIFE 9.1/10 (327); Campendium 4.1 (13). Some I-65 noise, some uneven gravel.
Notes
Road construction on Three Springs Rd has removed some signage. source

Louisville May 2–May 4, 2 nights

First callcandidate

Louisville South KOA Holiday

(502) 543-2041

2433 Hwy 44 E, Shepherdsville, KY

Sites
148 full-hookup RV sites; 30/50 A; pull-throughs up to 90 ft
Groups
10+ sites counts as a group; staff can put sites together; pavilion seats 30. Derby is a special event: 2-night minimum, cancel 2 weeks ahead or lose the deposit.
Drive
About 25 min to Churchill Downs (I)
Reviews
RV LIFE 8.2/10 (417); Campendium 4.0 (32). About $80/night reported Mar 2026.
Notes
Ask for Derby-week rates and minimum stays in writing. source

Elkhorn Creek RV Park (was Elkhorn Campground)

(502) 695-9154

165 N Scruggs Ln, Frankfort, KY

Sites

Lexington horse country May 7–May 13, 6 nights

First callcandidate

Kentucky Horse Park Campground

Groups 20+: (859) 259-4257 or (800) 370-6416

4089 Iron Works Pike, Lexington, KY (mail: Attention: Campground)

Sites
260 paved back-ins, 55 ft; 20/30/50 A plus water; no sewer at sites (2 dump stations)
Groups
Groups of 20+ book by phone. Standard bookings open 1 year ahead. Pavilion rentable.
Drive
About 20 min to Keeneland (I)
Reviews
The Dyrt 4.0 (50); Campendium 4.1 (53): clean, mostly level; Loop B less level.
Notes
2012 park, same name and phone. Horse-trials weekend (Kentucky Three-Day Event) has a 5-night minimum; arriving May 7 avoids it. source
