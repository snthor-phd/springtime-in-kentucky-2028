Itinerary

22 nights at five bases, 247 towing miles. Bowling Green is on Central time; everything else is Eastern. Day-by-day schedules and directions come later.

  1. 1Apr 29–May 23 nights

    Bowling Green & Mammoth Cave Bowling Green, KY Central time

    Rendezvous. Arrive from home on your own schedule.

    Rendezvous and cave country. One base covers Mammoth Cave National Park and Bowling Green.

    • Mammoth Cave tours (book on Recreation.gov)
    • Hidden River Cave and the American Cave Museum
    • Welcome dinner and first drivers' meeting

    Campground: Bowling Green KOA Holiday, (270) 843-1919 candidate

  2. 2May 2–May 42 nights

    Louisville Shepherdsville, KY

    98 mi from Bowling Green & Mammoth Cave, about 2.2 h towing

    Pre-Derby race day, Wed May 3

    Derby week in Louisville: a day at the races at Churchill Downs before the crowds of Oaks and Derby.

    • Pre-Derby race day at Churchill Downs, Wed May 3
    • Kentucky Derby Museum
    • Bernheim Forest, 15 minutes from camp

    Campground: Louisville South KOA Holiday, (502) 543-2041 candidate

  3. 3May 4–May 73 nights

    Frankfort Frankfort, KY

    69 mi from Louisville, about 1.6 h towing

    Kentucky Derby at Keeneland, Sat May 6

    The capital and bourbon. Buffalo Trace up the road, and Derby Day at Keeneland about 30 minutes away.

    • Buffalo Trace Distillery, Trace Tour
    • Kentucky History Center and Old State Capitol
    • Derby Day at Keeneland, Sat May 6

    Campground: Elkhorn Creek RV Park (was Elkhorn Campground), (502) 695-9154 candidate

  4. 4May 7–May 136 nights

    Lexington horse country Kentucky Horse Park, Lexington, KY

    19 mi from Frankfort, about 0.5 h towing

    Six nights among the horse farms, camped at the Kentucky Horse Park.

    • Kentucky Horse Park museum and Hall of Champions
    • Working horse-farm tours
    • Camp Nelson National Monument

    Campground: Kentucky Horse Park Campground, Groups 20+: (859) 259-4257 or (800) 370-6416 candidate

  5. 5May 13–May 218 nights

    Berea & Renfro Valley Mount Vernon / Berea, KY

    61 mi from Lexington horse country, about 1.4 h towing

    Folk arts, mountain music and the farewell.

    • Kentucky Artisan Center and Berea's craft galleries
    • Renfro Valley shows (check the 2028 schedule)
    • Farewell, Sun May 21

    Campground: Renfro Valley KOA Holiday, (606) 256-2474 candidate

  6. ★May 21farewell

    Farewell

    Sun May 21, 2028. Venue to be set.
